# MONO Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 2026

MONO is a minimalist, privacy-first social and group bill-splitting application.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** Username, display name, email address, and securely salted/hashed password (`scrypt`).
- **User Content:** Still photos, captions, hashtags, optional location labels, comments, 24-hour stories, direct/group messages, and group bill-splitting records.
- **Activity Status (Optional):** Last active timestamp, which you can disable at any time in Profile → Settings.

## 2. Automatic Data Erasure
- **Messages:** Every direct and group message is automatically and permanently erased from our servers **30 days** after it is sent.
- **Stories:** Every story is automatically and permanently erased **24 hours** after publication.

## 3. Private Social Graph
- Follower and following lists are strictly private and never displayed to other users or third parties.
- We do not run Reels, algorithmic engagement tracking, or third-party advertising SDKs.

## 4. Safety & Moderation
- You can block any user or report abusive posts, comments, stories, or messages directly in the app.

## 5. Account & Data Deletion
- You may permanently delete your account and all associated content at any time inside the app via **Profile → Settings → Delete Account Permanently**.
- Deletion immediately revokes active sessions and removes your profile, posts, stories, messages, and comments.

## 6. Contact
For privacy or safety inquiries, contact the app founder at `privacy@mono.app`.
